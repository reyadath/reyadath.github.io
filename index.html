Publications

Data-Diversity-Aware Quantization for Semi-Synchronous Federated Deep Reinforcement Learning in Vehicular Edge Computing

Under Review: 1st Major Revision Submitted at Future Generation Computer Systems · 2026

Introduces a data-diversity-aware quantization framework (DQS-FeDRL) that dynamically compresses client updates based on their cohort-relative informational novelty, integrated with a Bayesian availability-aware scheduler to distinguish between transient stragglers and permanent dropouts. This approach prevents training stalls, achieving up to 2.9× faster wall-clock convergence and a 75% reduction in communication overhead in dynamic vehicular networks.

Ittehad Saleh Chowdhury, Reyadath Ullah, Md. Abdur Razzaque, Abdulhameed Alelaiwi, Md. Rafiul Hassan, Mohammad Mehedi Hassan.

Federated Deep Reinforcement Learning for Optimal Resource Allocation in Vehicular Edge Computing

SAI Computing Conference 2025 · London

PDF

Proposes a Twin Delayed Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (TD3) based scheduler to optimize device selection and bandwidth allocation. It prioritizes clients with diverse datasets rather than just fast network connections to improve model generalization, demonstrating a 5% improvement in model accuracy alongside a 38% reduction in edge device energy consumption.

Reyadath Ullah, Ittehad Chowdhury, Md. Abdur Razzaque, Palash Roy, Mohammad Mehedi Hassan.

Reducing Complexity in Exporting RMG Products Using AI in Bangladesh

Supply Chain Insider, Vol 12, Issue 01

Reyadath Ullah, Ahmed Shakibul Islam, Toma Ray, Nusrat Najnen Eva.
